# Uplink

The framework's [chat](https://github.com/derlocke-ng/kiwi-framework/tree/main/apps/chat) app, mounted here as Uplink: messages to your friends (NIP-17, sealed on your device), groups that stay until their host closes them, and live rooms anyone with the link can join, gone when they empty; replies, reactions, edits, pinned messages and conversations, a search on the device and disappearing messages (0.30); voice and video calls (0.31). This folder holds only what Armory lays over it (the icon). It takes over from EnigmaJS, the old rooms on gun.
